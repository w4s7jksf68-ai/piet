macOS builds use Apple Accelerate, Cocoa and WebKit provided by the operating
system. Their implementations are not redistributed in this source release.

6. Data and excluded implementations
The included sample is synthetic project-created data. Third-party research
samples and reference-software logs are not included. The first public beta
omits implementations of egen concat, egen cut and business-calendar creation
because their development records identify transcription of official Stata ado
code. Stata EULA section 2.5 does not provide a general redistribution grant for
an independent GPL application: https://www.stata.com/order/end-user-license-agreement/19eula2025.pdf
This is a release-scope decision, not a legal opinion on all interoperability.
