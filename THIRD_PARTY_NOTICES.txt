Bundled sample dataset — provenance
-----------------------------------

"Fatalities" — US traffic fatalities panel data, 48 US states, annual
1982–1988 (336 observations). Bundled with this application as
Resources/sample/fatalities.csv for demonstration and teaching.

The variables are compiled from United States government statistics:
traffic fatality counts from the US Department of Transportation NHTSA
Fatality Analysis Reporting System (FARS), with demographic and economic
covariates from the US Census Bureau and the Bureau of Economic Analysis,
plus state alcohol policy variables. As factual data compiled from US
government sources, the dataset contents are public-domain facts.

The panel was assembled for and analyzed in:
  Ruhm, C. J. (1996). "Alcohol policies and highway vehicle fatalities."
  Journal of Health Economics, 15(4), 435-454.
It is widely used as a teaching dataset (e.g. Stock & Watson,
"Introduction to Econometrics") and is distributed in the R package "AER"
(Kleiber & Zeileis), from which this CSV copy was obtained via the
Rdatasets collection (https://vincentarelbundock.github.io/Rdatasets/).

Rules (authority: the project's distribution policy, sections 1.b, 3.2, 3.3):

  * Every adoption carries a source-file header comment in this exact form:
        // Portions derived from <project> <version-or-commit> (<license>).
        // See THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.
  * Every adoption adds a row to the register below IN THE SAME COMMIT that
    lands the code. A header without a row, or a row without a header, is a
    release-gate failure (the packaging preflight greps both directions).
  * "Consulted" is not "adopted": reading a source to confirm a formula, and
    then writing our own implementation, is recorded in the source comment as
    a method reference and does NOT belong in this register. Copying,
    transliterating, or closely tracking the structure of someone's code DOES.
  * Compatible to adopt: GPL-3.0, GPL-2.0-or-later, LGPL, MPL-2.0, Apache-2.0,
    MIT, BSD, BSL-1.0, and other GPL-3-compatible licenses.
  * NOT adoptable, escalate instead: GPL-2.0-only, AGPL, SSPL, and anything
    whose license cannot be established. An SSC .ado with no license file and
    no license statement anywhere is in this class; one whose SSC/RePEc
    listing states its license (lassopack, swindex: GPL v3) is adoptable,
    and its notice file records that the license is stated in the listing.
